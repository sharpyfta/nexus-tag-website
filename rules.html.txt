  <section class="section">
    <h1>Community Rules</h1>
    <p class="rules-intro">
      To keep Nexus Tag fun, fair, and welcoming for everyone, all players
      must follow the rules listed below. Failure to follow these rules may
      result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the game.
    </p>

    <h2>1. Respect All Players</h2>
    <p>
      Treat every player with respect. Harassment, bullying, targeting,
      threatening behavior, or encouraging harm toward others is strictly
      prohibited. Nexus Tag is a community-driven game, and respect is required
      at all times.
    </p>

    <h2>2. No Profanity or Offensive Language</h2>
    <p>
      Do not use profanity, hate speech, slurs, or offensive language of any kind.
      This includes language related to race, gender, sexuality, religion,
      disability, or nationality. Keep voice chat and text communication clean.
    </p>

    <h2>3. No Cheating or Exploits</h2>
    <p>
      Cheating, hacking, exploiting bugs, using mods that provide unfair
      advantages, or manipulating game mechanics is not allowed. If you find
      a bug, report it instead of abusing it.
    </p>

    <h2>4. Play Fair</h2>
    <p>
      Do not intentionally ruin matches for others. This includes spawn camping,
      griefing, blocking players, or deliberately interfering with gameplay
      to cause frustration.
    </p>

    <h2>5. No Impersonation</h2>
    <p>
      Do not impersonate developers, moderators, or other players. Pretending
      to have authority you do not have will result in punishment.
    </p>

    <h2>6. Follow VR Safety Guidelines</h2>
    <p>
      Be aware of your surroundings while playing. Nexus Tag is not responsible
      for injuries caused by unsafe play environments. Always play responsibly.
    </p>

    <h2>7. Reporting Issues</h2>
    <p>
      If you experience rule-breaking behavior or technical issues, report it
      to the creator. Do not retaliate or escalate situations in-game.
    </p>

    <h2>Contact</h2>
    <p>
      For questions, reports, or concerns, contact the owner and creator at:  
      <strong>sharpzfta12@gmail.com</strong>
    </p>
